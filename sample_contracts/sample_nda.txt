MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on 1 November 2026 between Alpha Tech Pvt Ltd ("Disclosing Party") and Beta Solutions LLP ("Receiving Party").

1. Definition of Confidential Information. "Confidential Information" means all business, technical and financial information shared by the Disclosing Party, whether written, oral or electronic.

2. Confidentiality Obligations. The Receiving Party shall keep all Confidential Information secret, use it only for evaluating the proposed business relationship, and not disclose it to any third party. These obligations of confidentiality shall continue in perpetuity.

3. Term. This Agreement remains in effect for a period of 2 years from the date written above.

4. Termination. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving 7 days written notice to the other party.

5. Return of Information. Upon termination, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy all Confidential Information within 10 days. The Disclosing Party may, at its sole discretion, extend this deadline.

6. Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of India.

7. Dispute Resolution. Any dispute shall be settled by arbitration in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, and the courts of Ahmedabad shall have jurisdiction.

8. General. This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties and may be changed only in writing signed by both parties.
